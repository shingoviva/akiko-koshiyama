# Lycoris radiata model

Sources consulted 2026-10-16:
- Flora of China, Amaryllidaceae, Lycoris radiata: https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=3&taxon_id=200028063
- NC State Extension: https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/lycoris-radiata/
- User-supplied close-up and whole-flower reference photographs.

Flora of China describes an umbel of 4–7 florets, a green perianth tube about 5 mm long, and strongly reflexed, strongly undulate tepals about 30 × 5 mm. The model uses six florets, each with six tepals in two staggered whorls, six stamens and a separate style. Tepal centerline length is about 1.275 model units, maximum width approximately 0.2; the tube is 0.167 units. These are approximate botanical proportions, not a specimen scan.

Reconstructed details: recurved tepal tips, wavy margins, central fold, six exserted curved filaments, anthers, style, green floral tubes, inferior ovaries, pedicels and two dry involucral bracts. Flower posture, individual variation and stamen curvature are inferred from photographs. Existing motion, blur, lighting, opacity and spawning parameters are unchanged.
